## 6.4 Giant Creature/Fantasy Scene

**Technique**: Scale contrast + Stylization + Realistic scene fusion

```
Add a giant fantasy creature to a real city/scene.

Base Scene:
- Uploaded city/scene photo
- Maintain real buildings, streets, vehicles, and people
- Preserve photo realism

Added Elements:
- Giant creature: [Creature description]
- Position: Sky/Behind buildings/Above city
- Scale: Very large, overlooking entire city

Creature Design:
- Flat graphic style
- Clear silhouettes
- Limited neon colors (e.g., soft neon green, neon yellow, lime green)
- Similar to mural or poster illustration

Composition:
- Creature as background element, not blocking main subject
- Create dramatic scale contrast
- City provides scale and realism

Style:
- Real photo + Stylized illustration
- Fusion of reality and fantasy
- Illustration style: [Specific style description]
```

**Example**: Giant stylized creature above city, fantasy creature fused with cityscape
